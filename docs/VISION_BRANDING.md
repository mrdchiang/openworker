# Vision branding and demonstration sources

Brand reference: [MACOM public website](https://www.macom.com/), accessed September 9, 2026.

The bundled, unmodified [MACOM logo](../surfaces/gui/src/assets/macom-logo.svg) was obtained from:
https://www.macom.com/files/live/modules/macom-templates-2/1.1.7/templates/files/logo.svg

Colors observed in the public site's stylesheet: navy `#00263e`, logo blue `#005a84`, blue `#0076a8`, and cyan `#00b1c7`. Vision uses these as a visual reference, not as a claim to have an official internal brand guide. The public logo remains MACOM's mark; the upstream software license does not grant ownership of it.

All Project Aurora records, DEMO-RF-100 specifications, issue IDs, assets, repository activity, dates, and team names are invented for this pitch. They are not extracted from MACOM systems or real product documentation. The catalog and MCP responses label them as fictional. No live Jira, Microsoft 365, GitHub, or inventory account is configured by the demo installer.

The Okta settings saved during testing use `vision-demo.example.org` and `fictional-demo-client`. These are placeholders, not a tenant registration. No authentication flow is active.
